Sukkah 32a–36a · Orach Chaim 645–648

אַרְבַּעַת הַמִּינִיםThe Four Species as Plants

What each of the four species is actually doing as a plant, set against what the sugya asks of it. Grafting versus cross-breeding versus cross-pollination in the esrog and what DNA can and cannot see; why the pitom stays or falls; what makes a Deri lulav a Deri; how rare a truly three-leaved myrtle is and why; the willow's field key; a blemish guide that reads black dots, brown scars, chazazis and scratches region by region; and the esrog lineages, orchard by orchard, with what was claimed against each and who relied on it.

This is a map of the sugya, not a psak. Each card puts the botany, the horticulture and the genetics beside the Mishnah, the Gemara and the Shulchan Aruch, and names the she'eilah where they meet without deciding it. Every source is quoted as written and linked; the plant science is linked to its studies.
Sources quoted verbatim from the Wm. Davidson Talmud, the Mishnah, and the Shulchan Aruch with Rema and Magen Avraham; contemporary teshuvos and studies are linked, not adjudicated.
