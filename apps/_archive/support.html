Torah Interactive is a personal project, built to make the inyanim that are easier to see than to read a little clearer — every source brought verbatim and linked to where it lives.

It is given freely and stays free. If it has been of use to your learning and you would like to help it grow — more apps, deeper sourcing, and keeping it running — a contribution goes directly toward that avodah. It is a way to have a share in harbatzas Torah. Every amount is appreciated; none is expected.
