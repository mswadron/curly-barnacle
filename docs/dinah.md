# How Old Was Dinah? · בת כמה היתה דינה

**Seder:** Chumash
**Tile:** [apps/dinah.html](../apps/dinah.html) (+ `dinah-data.js`, `dinah.js`)
**Status:** Live (linked from index.html), added 2026-10-21

## What it covers
Dinah's age at the incident of Shechem (Bereishis 34) treated as a set of links that must all hold at once, after Joshua Waxman, "How Old Was Dinah? A Constraint Satisfaction Problem, Explained" (Scribal Error, 2026-10-19). All Hebrew UI.

Sliders, each with its opinions marked on the track and quoted under it; a warning shows when the slider stands where no opinion holds:
0. When Yaakov married Leah: after the seven years (29:20-21, Seder Olam 2) or at the start of the service (Ralbag on Torah, Genesis 29:1, proven from Yehuda's sons). Added 2026-10-22.
1. Time between births: 6 months (Gur Aryeh; "the ancients" in Ibn Ezra 30:23), 7 months (Seder Olam 2; Karnei Ohr for Ibn Ezra), 9 months (no explicit source; Ralbag 29:1 spreads the births over fourteen years, which implies ordinary pregnancies, flagged as inference).
1b. Pause after Yehuda ("ותעמוד מלדת", 29:35): none (Seder Olam, inferred from continuous 7-month births) or at least 2 years (Karnei Ohr). Added 2026-10-22.
1c. Did Yissachar wait for Zilpah's sons: in the order of the story (30:9 then 30:14, inferred) or overlapping births (Ibn Ezra 30:23, inferred). Added 2026-10-22.
2. Time from leaving Lavan to Shechem: 18 months (Megillah 17a, Seder Olam 2, Rashi 33:17, Gur Aryeh); "many years" (Ibn Ezra 33:20, open band, flagged as inference).
3. Levi's minimum age: 13 (Bereishis Rabbah 80:10, Gur Aryeh; Ibn Ezra by inference). All agree.
4. Yosef's birth year: end of year 14 (Bereishis 31:41, Megillah 17a, Seder Olam 2). All agree.
5. Dinah's birth in the order: after Zevulun (30:21) or unknown (Ibn Ezra 30:23).
6. Dinah's age: about 6 (Soferim 21:9, at Osnat's birth), 8y1m (Rabbeinu Bachya 34:3), 6y11m (Karnei Ohr explaining Ibn Ezra; his count stops at year 20 and does not add the 18 months in Sukkos), 7 and 9 or 10 (Seder HaDorot 2206), over 7 (Ibn Ezra by inference), 12 (naarah read halachically; no source, flagged). A bar above the slider shows the range the other choices allow.

Presets: Rashi/Gemara with ordinary pregnancy (no solution, Levi 12 1/4), Seder Olam (no solution, Levi 12 3/4), Gur Aryeh (Dinah 7 1/2 to 10 1/2), Ibn Ezra (5 years used as an example for "many years"), Ralbag (wedding at the start, ordinary pregnancy: Levi 19 1/4, Dinah 7 1/2 to 15 1/2; the 18 months are from the Gemara, not Ralbag).

## Sources (verbatim, linked)
Bereishis 30:21, 31:41, 34:3, 34:25; Rashi 33:17; Bereishis Rabbah 80:10; Megillah 17a; Seder Olam Rabbah 2; Tractate Soferim 21:9; Ibn Ezra 30:23, 33:20; Gur Aryeh 33:17; Ralbag on Torah, Genesis 29:1; Rabbeinu Bachya 34:3; Bereishis 29:20-21; Karnei Ohr (Mechokekei Yehudah) 33:27; Seder HaDorot 2206.

## Method
Years counted from Yaakov's arrival at Lavan. Reuven at the wedding year (7, or 0 for Ralbag) + one gap; Leah's first four in a row; Gad after Yehuda; Yissachar at Yehuda + one gap + pause, and not before Asher unless births overlap; Zevulun then Dinah (or Dinah free after the wedding, up to year 20). Shechem at 20 + stay. Levi's age uses his earliest birth.
