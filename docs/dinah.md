# How Old Was Dinah? · בת כמה היתה דינה

**Seder:** Chumash
**Tile:** [apps/dinah.html](../apps/dinah.html) (+ `dinah-data.js`, `dinah.js`)
**Status:** Live (linked from index.html), added 2026-10-21

## What it covers
Dinah's age at the incident of Shechem (Bereishis 34) treated as a set of links that must all hold at once, after Joshua Waxman, "How Old Was Dinah? A Constraint Satisfaction Problem, Explained" (Scribal Error, 2026-10-19). All Hebrew UI.

Sliders, each with its opinions marked on the track and quoted under it; a warning shows when the slider stands where no opinion holds:
1. Time between births: 6 months (Gur Aryeh; "the ancients" in Ibn Ezra 30:23), 7 months (Seder Olam 2; Karnei Ohr for Ibn Ezra), 9 months (no source, flagged).
2. Time from leaving Lavan to Shechem: 18 months (Megillah 17a, Seder Olam 2, Rashi 33:17, Gur Aryeh); "many years" (Ibn Ezra 33:20, open band, flagged as inference).
3. Levi's minimum age: 13 (Bereishis Rabbah 80:10, Gur Aryeh; Ibn Ezra by inference). All agree.
4. Yosef's birth year: end of year 14 (Bereishis 31:41, Megillah 17a, Seder Olam 2). All agree.
5. Dinah's birth in the order: after Zevulun (30:21) or unknown (Ibn Ezra 30:23).
6. Dinah's age: about 6 (Soferim 21:9, at Osnat's birth), 6y11m (Karnei Ohr computing under Rashi), 7 and 9 or 10 (Seder HaDorot 2206), over 7 (Ibn Ezra by inference), 12 (naarah read halachically; no source, flagged). A bar above the slider shows the range the other choices allow.

Presets: Rashi/Gemara with ordinary pregnancy (no solution, Levi 12 1/4), Seder Olam (no solution, Levi 12 3/4), Gur Aryeh (Dinah 7 1/2 to 10 1/2), Ibn Ezra (5 years used as an example for "many years").

## Sources (verbatim, linked)
Bereishis 30:21, 31:41, 34:3, 34:25; Rashi 33:17; Bereishis Rabbah 80:10; Megillah 17a; Seder Olam Rabbah 2; Tractate Soferim 21:9; Ibn Ezra 30:23, 33:20; Gur Aryeh 33:17; Karnei Ohr (Mechokekei Yehudah) 33:27; Seder HaDorot 2206.

## Method
Years counted from Yaakov's arrival at Lavan. Reuven at 7 + one gap; Leah's first four in a row; Gad after Yehuda; Yissachar not before Asher; Zevulun then Dinah (or Dinah free after the wedding, up to year 20). Shechem at 20 + stay. Levi's age uses his earliest birth.

## Open questions
- No commentator found who reads naarah (34:3) as making Dinah 12; it is the article's suggestion.
- No source found stating 9-month gaps between the shevatim.
- Not in the calculation, shown as text: Ibn Ezra 30:23 overlapping births; Karnei Ohr's minimum two years for "ותעמוד מלדת".
- Travel time from Charan to Sukkos is not counted.
