# Tolaim in Food · תּוֹלָעִים בַּמַּאֲכָל

**Seder:** Kashrus
**Tile:** [apps/tolaim.html](../apps/tolaim.html)
**Status:** Live (linked from index.html)

## What it covers
Every insect and parasite that gets into food — in water, fish, produce, and meat — set against the sugya of tolaim: was it born in its place, or did it come from outside and separate. Each creature is placed in its category in the Gemara and Shulchan Aruch, the machlokes is left to the poskim, and a live scientific reading of the organism sits alongside.
